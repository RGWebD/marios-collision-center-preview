Trusted Local Collision Experts

Mario's Collision Center is a well-known auto body and collision shop in Augusta, GA. Customers consistently highlight Mario's humble, kind approach and the quality of the work his crew delivers.

From minor dents to full collision repairs (including a Tesla finished in just over a day), the shop is praised for transparency, competitive pricing, and results that look better than expected. One reviewer called the location "car repair paradise" once you make it down the road.
